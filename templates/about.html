The portal is organised district-first, because that is how people actually plan a trip in Karnataka: you pick a base, then look for what is within a two-hour drive. Every destination page carries the practical details - timings, entry fees, the best months, how to get there and what is nearby - along with photographs and a map.

Built as a final-year college project with Flask, SQLite, Jinja templates and OpenStreetMap. Photographs are sourced from Wikimedia Commons; destination facts are checked against public sources.

How it works

Four steps from browsing to a ticket

1. Explore

Search by district, category or keyword, or open the map and click a pin.

2. Plan

Add places to an itinerary and the planner orders them into days with distances.

3. Book

Pick a date and party size, then run through the demo payment screen.

4. Carry

Download the PDF ticket with its QR code, or print it from the browser.
