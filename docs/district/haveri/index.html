Haveri

Nagareshwara Temple, Bankapura

A 12th-century temple with a 24-pillared hall and intricate carvings, standing inside the ruins of the Bankapura fort walls. The surrounding village was once a fortified city, and the temple's carved doorways and ceiling panels are among the finest in the Haveri region.

Ranebennur Blackbuck Sanctuary

A small sanctuary of open grassland and acacia scrub created to protect one of the largest blackbuck populations in Karnataka, along with wolf, jackal and the endangered Great Indian Bustard historically. Herds of blackbuck are easily seen from the approach road, especially in the early morning.

Tarakeshwara Temple, Hangal

An 11th-12th century Chalukyan temple with an unusually large and elaborate hall of 108 carved pillars, each different, and a stepped tank beside it. Hangal was a provincial capital of the Kalyani Chalukyas and the temple remains the town's centre, with a yearly fair and a lively market around it.
