Beaches ₹10
Udupi

Kaup Beach and Lighthouse

A rocky beach with a 27-metre lighthouse built in 1901 on a black rock outcrop, which can be climbed for a view of the coast. The beach is quieter than Malpe and the rocks and tide pools make it good for photography, especially at sunset.

Waterfalls ₹500
Udupi

Kudlu Theertha Falls

A waterfall inside the Someshwara Wildlife Sanctuary, reached by a forest trek of about 4 km from the gate near Hebri. The stream falls about 100 metres into a pool, and the walk through evergreen forest is as much the attraction as the falls. Entry is limited and a forest guide is required.

Temples Free entry
Udupi

Sri Krishna Temple, Udupi

One of south India's most visited temples, founded in the 13th century by the philosopher Madhvacharya, with the child Krishna idol said to have been hidden in clay by a devotee to save it from Portuguese raiders. The temple's Kanakana Kindi, a nine-holed window through which the saint Kanakadasa is said to have seen the deity, is a symbol of the temple's inclusiveness, and the adjoining math runs the famous Udupi kitchens.

Beaches ₹300
Udupi

St. Mary's Island

A small island of columnar basalt rocks off Malpe beach, formed by volcanic activity and compared to the Giant's Causeway in Northern Ireland. Reached by a 15-minute boat ride, it has a shell-strewn beach and a lighthouse, and the hexagonal rock columns are best seen at low tide. Snoopy Island nearby offers snorkelling.
