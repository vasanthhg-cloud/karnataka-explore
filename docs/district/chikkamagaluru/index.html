Chikkamagaluru

Karnataka's coffee country - Mullayanagiri, Hebbe Falls and shola forest.

Chikkamagaluru

Baba Budangiri

A mountain range and pilgrimage site where the Sufi saint Baba Budan is believed to have planted the first coffee seeds brought from Yemen, giving Karnataka its coffee country. A cave shrine and a Dargah sit side by side on the ridge, visited by both Hindus and Muslims, and the drive across the range passes through shola grassland of great botanical interest.

Chikkamagaluru

Hebbe Falls

A two-stage waterfall inside the Kemmangundi estate, reached by a jeep track and then a 2 km walk down through coffee and forest. The upper fall drops about 170 metres; the lower pool is cold, clear and deep, and swimming is allowed with care. Leeches are common in the monsoon, and the walk is steep on the way back.

Chikkamagaluru

Hirekolale Lake

A small irrigation lake on the outskirts of Chikkamagaluru, backed by the Mullayanagiri and Baba Budangiri hills. The still water mirrors the mountains at sunset, and the surrounding fields and coffee estates make it a pleasant cycling and picnic stop. It is also a good place for spotting water birds.

National Parks ₹400
Chikkamagaluru

Kudremukh National Park

A 600 sq km national park of shola forest and grassland around the Kudremukh peak, once the site of a large iron-ore mine that has since closed and is being restored. It is a UNESCO World Heritage candidate within the Western Ghats and a stronghold of lion-tailed macaque, Malabar giant squirrel and gaur. Trekking is permitted only with Forest Department guides.

Chikkamagaluru

Mullayanagiri Peak

At 1,930 metres, the highest peak in Karnataka, reached by a road that winds up from Chikkamagaluru through coffee estates and shola forest. A further 500 steps from the road-end climb to the summit shrine and a panoramic view over the Baba Budangiri range and, on clear days, the Arabian Sea coast. Sunrise here is the classic Chikkamagaluru experience.
