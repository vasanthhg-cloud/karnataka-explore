Doddabasappa Temple, Dambal

A 12th-century temple with a rare 24-sided stellate plan and a dome built in corbelled rings, standing in a small village on the old trade route between Badami and Lakkundi. The carving is worn but the geometry of the plan is striking, and the site is usually completely empty of visitors.

Lakkundi Temples

A village of over 50 surviving temples and stepwells from the Kalyani Chalukya period, when Lakkundi was a great centre of art and trade. The Kasivisvesvara and Brahma Jinalaya temples show the finest soapstone carving in north Karnataka, and the ornate stepwells, called kalyani, are as impressive as the shrines.

Gadag

Trikuteshwara Temple, Gadag

An 11th-century Kalyani Chalukya temple with three shrines to Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva, and a hall of lathe-turned pillars considered among the best in the Deccan. The temple's Saraswati shrine and its carved ceilings reward a slow visit, and Gadag town is also the birthplace of the poet Kumaravyasa.
