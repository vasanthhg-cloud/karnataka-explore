Channapatna

A town of toy makers, known across India for its lacquered wooden toys turned on hand lathes and coloured with vegetable dyes. The craft has a GI tag and a 200-year history encouraged by Tipu Sultan, and several workshops and the government craft complex welcome visitors to watch the process.

Mekedatu

A narrow gorge where the Kaveri squeezes between granite boulders, its name meaning goat's leap. The river runs through a deep channel here and the surrounding scrub forest is part of a wildlife corridor; a temple stands on the bank. Access is regulated and the last stretch is a walk.

Ramanagara

Ramadevara Betta

A rocky hill above Ramanagara town with a temple to Rama at the top and the cliffs that were famously filmed as the backdrop of Sholay. The short climb gives a view over the town and the granite quarries, and vultures and other raptors are often seen riding the thermals along the cliff edge.

Ramanagara

Savandurga

One of the largest monolith hills in Asia, rising about 1,200 metres above sea level, with a fort at the top built by Kempe Gowda and later held by Tipu Sultan. The climb takes two to three hours up steep bare rock with fixed handrails in places, and the summit gives a view over the Arkavathi valley and the Manchanabele reservoir.
