Kolar

Ancient temples, the Antara Gange spring and the old gold fields.

Temples Free entry
Kolar

Antara Gange

A hill temple built over a natural spring that flows out of a cave in the granite, dedicated to Shiva as Antareshwara. The walk up the hill passes several small shrines and a tank, and the spring water is considered sacred. The hill also has a trekking path to the summit with views over Kolar.

Temples Free entry
Kolar

Kolaramma Temple

A temple of the Ganga and Chola periods dedicated to the goddess Kolaramma, with a stone chariot in front and inscriptions from the 10th-century Chola king Rajendra Chola. The sanctum holds a large image of the goddess with seven heads, and the temple is the ritual centre of the town.

Temples Free entry
Kolar

Someshwara Temple, Kolar

A large Vijayanagara-era temple with a towering gopura, a hall of carved pillars and a tank, rebuilt over a Chola foundation in the 14th-16th centuries. The outer walls carry rows of reliefs and the temple's scale makes it the most impressive monument in Kolar.
