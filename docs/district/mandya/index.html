Mandya

Brindavan Gardens, KRS

Terraced gardens laid out below the Krishnaraja Sagar dam by Sir Mirza Ismail in the 1930s, with fountains, canals and an amphitheatre. The musical fountain show at dusk, when water jets dance to music under coloured light, is the main draw, and the gardens are a standard day trip from Mysuru.

Temples Free entry
Mandya

Melukote

A hill town of temples and stepwells built by the Srivaishnava teacher Ramanujacharya in the 12th century, with the Cheluvanarayana Swamy and Yoga Narasimha temples on and below the rock. The town is also known for its Sanskrit academy and for puliyogare, the tamarind rice sold by the temple shops. The view from the hill over the Kaveri valley is wide and quiet.

Wildlife Sanctuaries ₹75
Mandya

Ranganathittu Bird Sanctuary

A group of riverine islets on the Kaveri near Srirangapatna, declared a bird sanctuary in 1940 on the advice of ornithologist Salim Ali. In the breeding season thousands of painted storks, open-billed storks, spoonbills, ibises and cormorants nest on the islets, and boat rides take visitors within metres of them. Marsh crocodiles bask on the rocks.

Historical Places Free entry
Mandya

Srirangapatna

The island capital of Tipu Sultan and before him of the Vijayanagara viceroys, ringed by the Kaveri. The Ranganathaswamy temple, Tipu's summer palace Daria Daulat Bagh with its painted walls, the Gumbaz mausoleum and the breach in the fort walls where Tipu fell in 1799 are all within a few kilometres.
