Kodagu

Coffee, mist and the Kaveri's source, with elephant camps on the river.

Kodagu

Abbey Falls

A waterfall on the Kaveri's tributary about 9 km from Madikeri, where the stream drops through coffee and spice plantations into a rocky pool. A hanging bridge opposite the falls gives a full view, and the walk down passes through cardamom and pepper vines. It is at its heaviest in the monsoon.

Kodagu

Raja's Seat

A garden and viewpoint on a ridge at the western edge of Madikeri where the Kodagu rajas are said to have watched the sunset over their kingdom. The terraced garden looks out over the Western Ghats, and the valley below fills with mist in the evenings. A toy train and a musical fountain keep children occupied.

Adventure Tourism ₹100
Kodagu

Dubare Elephant Camp

A forest camp on the Kaveri where rescued and working elephants are cared for by the Forest Department, and visitors can help with feeding and bathing under supervision. Coracle rides on the river, river-side walks and the nearby Nisargadhama island make it a good family stop between Mysuru and Madikeri.

Mandalpatti

A grassland peak about 1,600 metres high, reached by a rough jeep track from Madikeri through coffee estates. The top opens into rolling shola grassland with views across the Western Ghats, and it is a favourite for off-road drives and sunrise trips.

Kodagu

Talakaveri

The source of the Kaveri river, high on the Brahmagiri hill, where a tank and a temple mark the spring that feeds one of south India's great rivers. Steps lead up to the Brahmagiri peak for a view over the surrounding ranges, and the site is busy during the Kaveri Sankramana festival in October.
