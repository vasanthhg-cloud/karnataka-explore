Waterfalls ₹30
Kodagu

Abbey Falls

A waterfall on the Kaveri's tributary about 9 km from Madikeri, where the stream drops through coffee and spice plantations into a rocky pool. A hanging bridge opposite the falls gives a full view, and the walk down passes through cardamom and pepper vines. It is at its heaviest in the monsoon.

Waterfalls ₹20
Shivamogga

Jog Falls

India's second-highest plunge waterfall, where the Sharavati drops about 253 metres in four distinct cascades named Raja, Rani, Roarer and Rocket. It is spectacular in the monsoon when the falls run full, and in the dry season the river is diverted to the Linganamakki reservoir and the flow is much reduced. A long flight of steps leads from the viewpoint down to the base.

Hill Stations ₹15
Chikkaballapura

Nandi Hills

A 1,478-metre granite hill that was Tipu Sultan's summer retreat and is now Bengaluru's favourite sunrise drive. Tipu's Drop, a 600-metre cliff, the old summer palace, the Bhoga Nandishwara temple at the foot and the mist that settles on the ridge at dawn are the main draws. The hill fort has been restored and the road to the top is now metalled.

Historical Places ₹25
Bagalkote

Badami Cave Temples

Four rock-cut cave temples carved into the sandstone cliffs above Agastya Lake between the 6th and 8th centuries, when Badami was the capital of the Early Chalukyas. Cave 1 is Shaiva, Caves 2 and 3 are Vaishnava and Cave 4 is Jain. Cave 3, dated 578 CE, holds some of the finest early Indian sculpture, including the eighteen-armed Nataraja and Vishnu seated on Ananta. The red sandstone cliffs and the lake below make the whole complex remarkably photogenic.

Historical Places ₹25
Vijayapura

Gol Gumbaz

The mausoleum of Muhammad Adil Shah, completed in 1656, with the second-largest pre-modern dome in the world after St. Peter's. The Whispering Gallery at the base of the dome carries a whisper across 37 metres, and the building's dark basalt and simple geometry make it one of the finest pieces of Deccan architecture.

Temples ₹10
Uttara Kannada

Murudeshwar Temple

A temple town on the coast where a 37-metre statue of Shiva stands on a hill above the sea, with a 20-storey gopura behind it. The temple is believed to hold a piece of the Atma Linga from the Ramayana story, and the lift inside the gopura takes visitors up for a view along the coastline.

Historical Places ₹100
Mysuru

Mysore Palace

The seat of the Wodeyar dynasty and one of India's most visited monuments after the Taj Mahal. The present palace was completed in 1912 by Henry Irwin after a fire, in a blend of Hindu, Islamic, Gothic and Rajput styles, with a 145-foot dome. Inside are the Durbar Hall, the golden throne and a large collection of paintings; on Sunday evenings and during Dasara the whole facade is lit with nearly 100,000 bulbs.

Nature and Eco-Tourism ₹50
Mandya

Brindavan Gardens, KRS

Terraced gardens laid out below the Krishnaraja Sagar dam by Sir Mirza Ismail in the 1930s, with fountains, canals and an amphitheatre. The musical fountain show at dusk, when water jets dance to music under coloured light, is the main draw, and the gardens are a standard day trip from Mysuru.

Forts ₹25
Chitradurga

Chitradurga Fort

A massive seven-walled fort built in stages by the Nayakas of Chitradurga between the 15th and 18th centuries, wrapping around a cluster of granite hills. Its 19 gateways, 38 bastions and ingenious water tanks are cut straight into the rock, and the Onake Obavva kindi, a narrow crevice in the wall, commemorates the woman who defended it single-handed. The fort was taken by Hyder Ali in 1779.
