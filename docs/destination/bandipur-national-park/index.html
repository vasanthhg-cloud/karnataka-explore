Bandipur National Park

Bandipur, on the Mysuru-Ooty highway Chamarajanagara ★★★☆☆ 3.0 (1) 3 hours (safari)

About Bandipur National Park

Part of the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve, this 870 sq km park of teak and sandalwood forest is one of India's strongest tiger reserves and holds healthy populations of elephant, gaur, dhole and leopard. Safari vehicles run morning and evening along the park's forest roads, and the Moyar gorge on the Tamil Nadu side marks the park's southern edge.

Chamarajanagara

Himavad Gopalaswamy Betta

The highest hill in the Bandipur range at about 1,450 metres, topped by a temple to Krishna as Gopalaswamy that sits inside a small fort. The drive up passes through forest, and on clear days the view over the Wayanad and Nilgiri ranges is superb. The hill is famous for the mist that rolls over it for much of the year, which gives it the name Himavad, or snow-capped.

Bharachukki Falls

The eastern branch of the Kaveri at Shivanasamudra, where the river breaks into a wide curtain of falls about 90 metres high. Coracle rides run to the base of the falls in season, and the viewpoint above gives a full view of the gorge. The western branch nearby is Gaganachukki; together they are among the largest falls in the state.

Biligirirangan Hills (BR Hills)

A hill range where the Western and Eastern Ghats meet, covered in dry deciduous forest and home to the Soliga tribal community. The BRT Tiger Reserve protects tiger, elephant and gaur, and the hill-top Biligiri Ranganatha temple draws pilgrims. The drive up from Yelandur through the forest is one of the prettiest in Karnataka.

Kudremukh National Park

A 600 sq km national park of shola forest and grassland around the Kudremukh peak, once the site of a large iron-ore mine that has since closed and is being restored. It is a UNESCO World Heritage candidate within the Western Ghats and a stronghold of lion-tailed macaque, Malabar giant squirrel and gaur. Trekking is permitted only with Forest Department guides.
