Gokarna, Uttara Kannada Uttara Kannada Half day

About Om Beach, Gokarna

A beach shaped like the Om symbol, with two crescents of sand and rocky headlands, reached by a short walk from Gokarna town. It is the most accessible of Gokarna's five beaches, with shacks, water sports and a steady flow of travellers, and the cliff path to Half Moon and Paradise beaches is a fine walk.

How to reach

Gokarna is 60 km from Karwar with a railway station on the Konkan line. Om Beach is 3 km from town by auto.

Temples ₹10
Uttara Kannada

Murudeshwar Temple

A temple town on the coast where a 37-metre statue of Shiva stands on a hill above the sea, with a 20-storey gopura behind it. The temple is believed to hold a piece of the Atma Linga from the Ramayana story, and the lift inside the gopura takes visitors up for a view along the coastline.

Uttara Kannada

Yana Caves

Two towering black karst rock formations, Bhairaveshwara Shikhara and Mohini Shikhara, rising out of evergreen forest in the Sahyadri range. A temple sits in the cave at the base of the larger rock, and the 2 km trek from the road head passes a stream and a forest shrine. It is a major draw for trekkers and photographers.

Waterfalls Free entry
Uttara Kannada

Unchalli Falls (Lushington Falls)

A 116-metre waterfall on the Aghanashini river, discovered in 1845 by the British officer J.D. Lushington. The falls are reached by a short forest walk from the road, and the viewpoint looks straight down into the gorge. The volume is enormous just after the monsoon.

Wildlife Sanctuaries ₹100
Uttara Kannada

Dandeli Wildlife Sanctuary

A 475 sq km sanctuary of moist deciduous forest on the Kali river, known for its white-water rafting, coracle rides, river cruises and sightings of hornbills, Malabar pied hornbill and the black panther. The Kali Tiger Reserve around it protects a large contiguous forest block.
