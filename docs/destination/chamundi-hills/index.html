Mysuru taluk Mysuru 2 hours

About Chamundi Hills

A hill of 1,000 steps crowned by the Chamundeshwari temple, the tutelary goddess of the Wodeyar kings, with a monolithic Nandi bull halfway up. The view over Mysuru from the top is the best in the city, and the climb through the shaded steps is a popular early-morning exercise.

Historical Places ₹100
Mysuru

Mysore Palace

The seat of the Wodeyar dynasty and one of India's most visited monuments after the Taj Mahal. The present palace was completed in 1912 by Henry Irwin after a fire, in a blend of Hindu, Islamic, Gothic and Rajput styles, with a 145-foot dome. Inside are the Durbar Hall, the golden throne and a large collection of paintings; on Sunday evenings and during Dasara the whole facade is lit with nearly 100,000 bulbs.

Mysuru

Mysore Zoo (Sri Chamarajendra Zoological Gardens)

One of the oldest zoos in India, opened in 1892 on 157 acres, and widely regarded as among the best maintained in the country. It is known for its conservation breeding of Indian elephants, giraffes and Indian rhinos, and for a large collection of birds and primates in open enclosures.

Mysuru

St. Philomena's Cathedral

A neo-Gothic cathedral built in 1936 on the model of Cologne Cathedral, with twin 175-foot spires and stained glass windows depicting the life of Christ. It is one of the tallest churches in India and is lit dramatically in the evenings.

Uttara Kannada

Murudeshwar Temple

A temple town on the coast where a 37-metre statue of Shiva stands on a hill above the sea, with a 20-storey gopura behind it. The temple is believed to hold a piece of the Atma Linga from the Ramayana story, and the lift inside the gopura takes visitors up for a view along the coastline.
