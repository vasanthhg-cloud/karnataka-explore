Kittur Fort and Palace

Kittur town, Bailhongal taluk Belagavi 1-2 hours

About Kittur Fort and Palace

The ancestral fort of Rani Chennamma, the queen who resisted the East India Company in 1824, decades before 1857. The fort ruins, the Rani Chennamma memorial and a small museum with weapons and period objects sit amid farmland about 50 km from Belagavi. A larger-than-life statue of the queen on horseback stands at the entrance.

Gokak Falls

The Ghataprabha river drops about 52 metres over a horseshoe-shaped sandstone cliff just outside Gokak town, giving one of the widest waterfalls in Karnataka. It is at its best in the monsoon and immediately after, when the whole rock face is white with water. A hanging suspension bridge over the gorge, a Chalukyan temple on the cliff, and the 1887 Gokak Mills hydro-electric station add interest.

Belagavi

Belagavi Fort

A sprawling fort begun in the 12th century by the Ratta dynasty and rebuilt many times, with stone walls, a moat and two deep wells cut into the rock. Inside are the Kamal Basadi and Chikki Basadi Jain temples with fine carved pillars, plus two mosques built during the Adil Shahi and Maratha periods, making it a compact lesson in the Deccan's layered history.

Godchinamalaki Falls

A two-tier waterfall on the Markandeya river about 16 km from Gokak, where the stream falls roughly 25 metres in two stages into a wooded gorge. It is quieter than Gokak Falls and far less visited, with a short forest walk to the viewpoint and a temple at the top of the falls.

Historical Places ₹25
Bagalkote

Badami Cave Temples

Four rock-cut cave temples carved into the sandstone cliffs above Agastya Lake between the 6th and 8th centuries, when Badami was the capital of the Early Chalukyas. Cave 1 is Shaiva, Caves 2 and 3 are Vaishnava and Cave 4 is Jain. Cave 3, dated 578 CE, holds some of the finest early Indian sculpture, including the eighteen-armed Nataraja and Vishnu seated on Ananta. The red sandstone cliffs and the lake below make the whole complex remarkably photogenic.
