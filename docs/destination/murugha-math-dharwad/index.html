About Murugha Math, Dharwad

A major Lingayat monastery in Dharwad, known for its library, its educational institutions and its role in the 12th-century Sharana tradition. The math is also a cultural centre for Hindustani music, and Dharwad itself is famous for its vocalists and for Dharwad peda.

Dharwad

Unkal Lake

A 200-acre lake on the edge of Hubballi with a garden, a walking path and a statue of Swami Vivekananda on an island reached by a footbridge. Sunsets over the water are the attraction, and the lake is a short drive from the twin cities' railway station.

Dharwad

Nrupatunga Betta

A low hill inside Hubballi named after the Rashtrakuta king Nrupatunga, with a park, an amphitheatre and a viewpoint over the city. It is the standard evening walk for Hubballi residents and gives a good sense of the city's layout between the hills.

Cultural Heritage ₹25
Bagalkote

Pattadakal Group of Monuments

A UNESCO World Heritage Site where the Chalukya kings built a row of temples in the 7th and 8th centuries to mark their coronations. Nine Hindu temples and a Jain sanctuary stand together on the bank of the Malaprabha, showing North Indian and South Indian styles side by side in the same compound. The Virupaksha Temple is the largest and still active; the Papanatha and Sangameshwara temples show the experiment of blending both traditions.

Bengaluru Rural

Nrityagram Dance Village

A residential dance village founded in 1990 by Odissi dancer Protima Gauri, where students live and train in classical Indian dance in mud-and-thatch buildings set among fields. Visitors can tour the campus, watch morning practice, and attend the annual Vasanta Habba festival held in the adjoining gardens.
